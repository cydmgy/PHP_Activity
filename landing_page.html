Our Philosophy

The Artistry Behind Every Elemental World

Developed by HoYoverse (miHoYo) and first released globally in 2020, Genshin Impact stands as a landmark open-world action RPG. Every windswept plain, jade mountain, and elemental burst is crafted with painterly detail and reverence for its sprawling fantasy setting.

Elemental Reactions

A signature combat system where Pyro, Hydro, Electro, Cryo, Anemo, Geo, and Dendro Visions combine to trigger Overloaded, Vaporize, and dozens of other reactions.

The Traveler's Journey

A silent protagonist searching across seven nations for a lost twin, meeting archons and unraveling the mystery of Celestia along the way.

Symphonic Storytelling

Sweeping orchestral themes composed by Yu-Peng Chen and the HOYO-MiX team, giving each nation its own unmistakable musical identity.

Venti's Melody

Disguised as a wandering bard strumming songs in Mondstadt's taverns, Venti is secretly Barbatos, the Anemo Archon who values freedom above all divine duty. He guides the Traveler through the city's political turmoil, never revealing his true identity until it truly matters.

Raiden Shogun's Journey

After the death of her twin sister Ei's original body, the Raiden Shogun created a puppet vessel to rule Inazuma and pursued an unchanging "Eternity" through the Vision Hunt Decree. Guided by the Traveler, she comes to accept that true eternity lies in cherished memories, not frozen perfection.

Key Symbol: The Musou Isshin, a blade said to cut through the illusion of unchanging time.

Zhongli's Tale

Once worshipped as Morax, the Geo Archon who laid Liyue's very mountains, Zhongli relinquished his Gnosis and now works as a modest funeral consultant. Beneath his unassuming exterior remains the strength that shaped a nation's contracts and its unshakable trust in commerce.

Key Symbol: Stone pillars and contracts, representing permanence and unbroken promises.

Nahida's Awakening

Sealed away for centuries within the dream realm as a vessel of Irminsul's knowledge, Nahida is freed by the Traveler and finally steps into Sumeru as its Dendro Archon. Young and curious about mortal life, she works to unite scholars and forest dwellers against the desert's growing Withering.
